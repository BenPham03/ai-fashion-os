# Business Rules

- BR-001: HITL campaigns cannot publish without approval.
- BR-002: Invalid/unvalidated video cannot be published.
- BR-003: The same content cannot be published twice on the same platform.
- BR-004: Unverified/nonexistent products must not become trusted persisted products.
- BR-005: AI output must pass schema and applicable business validation.
- BR-006: Workflows must support bounded retry and recovery.
- BR-007: Retries must not duplicate side effects.
- BR-008: Paused campaigns must not create new execution work.
- BR-009: Exceeding a configured hard AI budget pauses AI workflow execution.
- BR-010: Rejected content requires a reason.
- BR-011: Regeneration creates a new content version.
- BR-012: Raw analytics are immutable.
- BR-013: External writes are authorized and audited.
- BR-014: Agents cannot redefine business rules through prompts.
